How to Source Wholesale Children's Clothing from China

Follow a clear process to define the product, evaluate a supplier, request a quotation, confirm samples and specifications, plan the order and arrange international shipping.

Direct answer: Define your product and quantities first, identify a suitable supplier, evaluate its real products and capabilities, send a complete RFQ, confirm samples when needed, approve the final specifications and order terms, then confirm production or ready-stock allocation, quality expectations and shipping.

Start With the Product

Define Your Product Requirements First

Suppliers can only evaluate the request accurately when the product and commercial requirements are clear.

Category and age range

Identify the garment type and intended age or size range. A category alone may not explain the construction or fit required.

Quantity and breakdown

State the total quantity and the planned split by model, size and color when available.

Colors and materials

Describe color references and known fabric composition, weight, feel or performance requirements.

Design references

Attach photographs, sketches, technical files or an existing product model that explains the direction.

Customization

List printing, embroidery, private labels, size changes and packaging requirements separately.

Delivery context

Provide the target delivery date, destination country and any known shipping preference.

For a detailed field-by-field request and a copyable template, use the Wholesale Kidswear RFQ Guide.

Due Diligence

How to Evaluate a Children's Clothing Supplier

Evaluate evidence that is relevant to the proposed order instead of relying on promotional language alone.

Legal company information

Confirm the company name, contact details and business registration. Registration establishes a legal entity; it is not product-safety certification.

Catalog and real photographs

Review actual products, model references, sizes and photographs. Check whether the information remains consistent across the catalog and quotation.

Relevant capabilities

Ask which manufacturing, ready-stock, OEM, ODM, private-label, sample and customization services apply to the specific request.

Communication

Check whether the supplier asks relevant questions, explains limitations and records the agreed specifications clearly.

Sample and order terms

Clarify the sample purpose, MOQ, production terms, payment stages and approval steps before the main order is confirmed.

Quality and documentation

Ask how quality will be checked and which documents are relevant to the buyer's market. Do not treat a business licence, chamber membership or supplier award as product-safety certification.

Shipping and export support

Confirm available shipping methods, export-document handling and which party is responsible at each stage.

Market requirements

The buyer should identify the labeling, testing, import and sales requirements applicable in the destination market and request relevant evidence.

Verifiable details

Prefer precise model numbers, written specifications, named documents and confirmed commercial terms over unsupported claims.

Before Custom Production

Samples and Product Confirmation

A sample gives the buyer and supplier a physical reference for discussing the custom product before bulk production. Sample development is available from Yubei, but its purpose and requirements should be agreed for the actual project.

Depending on the request, the review can cover the design, sizing or measurements, color and material choices, printing or embroidery, brand labels and packaging details where applicable.

A sample does not replace a written product specification. Record approved details and any requested changes so the order is not based on memory or an informal chat alone. Sample prices, timing and policies must be confirmed directly; none are assumed in this guide.

Quantity Planning

MOQ and Order Planning

MOQ should be checked against the correct sourcing path. Yubei ready-stock products can be purchased in any quantity, subject to current inventory. The standard custom-production MOQ is 300 pieces per model.

For a custom project, clarify whether the MOQ applies by model, color or another breakdown before preparing the final order plan. Product specifications, customization and material availability should also be reviewed before a quotation or order is treated as final.

For ready stock, provide the exact model and size/color quantities so current availability can be checked.

Production and Quality Confirmation

Avoid Preventable Delays

Common Children's Clothing Sourcing Mistakes

Good sourcing is less about finding a shortcut and more about removing ambiguity before money, materials and time are committed.

Asking only for price

A price without product specifications, quantity and delivery context may not be comparable or useful.

Missing size breakdown

A total quantity does not show how many pieces are required in each size.

Unclear customization

Printing, embroidery, labels and packaging should be documented instead of assumed.

Mixing stock and custom orders

Ready stock depends on inventory; custom production depends on review, material availability and MOQ.

Not confirming MOQ

Confirm how MOQ applies to the specific model, colors and other order variables.

Leaving packaging until late

Packaging, labels, barcodes and carton marks can affect preparation and should be discussed early.

Delaying shipping decisions

The destination and shipping approach help define the logistics information needed for the order.

Relying on unsupported claims

Check what each document actually proves; registration and membership are not product-safety certification.

Approving only through chat

Use a final written specification and order summary to reduce disagreement about the accepted product.

Sourcing FAQ

Wholesale Children's Clothing Sourcing Questions

Supported answers to common questions from international buyers.

Can I buy children's clothing directly from China?

Yes. International buyers can source ready-stock or custom children's clothing directly from a supplier in China. Product requirements, commercial terms, shipping arrangements and the buyer's own import requirements should be confirmed before ordering.

What information should I send before asking for a quotation?

Send the product or model, quantity, sizes, colors, material requirements, customization, packaging, sample needs, target delivery date and destination. Attach a design or reference photograph when relevant.

What is the MOQ for custom Yubei kidswear?

Yubei's standard custom-production MOQ is 300 pieces per model. Product specifications and customization remain subject to technical review, material availability and the applicable MOQ.

Can I buy small quantities from ready stock?

Yes. Yubei ready-stock products can be purchased in any quantity, subject to current inventory. The requested models, sizes, colors and quantities should be checked when preparing the quotation.

Does Yubei provide OEM and ODM?

Yes. Yubei provides OEM, ODM, private-label and sample-development support. Custom fabric, color, printing, sizes and labels are available for reviewed projects.

Can Yubei add my brand label?

Yes. Buyers can include private-label requirements in a custom-production request and should provide the label type, placement, artwork and available specifications.

Can I send my own design or reference photo?

Yes. A design or reference photograph can be sent with the product specifications and required changes. Custom designs are subject to technical review, material availability and the applicable MOQ.

Can Yubei arrange international shipping?

Yes. International shipping can be arranged by sea or air, including door-to-door arrangements where applicable. Export-document handling is available.
