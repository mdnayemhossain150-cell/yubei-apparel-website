OEM & ODM Cooperation

Develop Custom Children's Clothing With Yubei

Huzhou Zhili Yubei Clothing Co., Ltd., also known as Yubei Apparel, works with buyers through OEM, ODM, custom design and private-label children's clothing cooperation. This page is for brands, wholesalers, distributors and other B2B buyers evaluating how Yubei can support a custom kidswear project.

As an OEM children's clothing manufacturer, Yubei can discuss production based on designs supplied by the buyer. When a buyer needs design support instead, the company's published ODM service allows its design team to discuss products based on buyer specifications and market requirements.

Buyers can review Yubei's broader kidswear manufacturing services or visit the About page for company, showroom and production-environment photographs.

Verified Customization

Fabric, Color, Size, Printing and Logo Options

Yubei's published customization capabilities include fabric, color, size and printing or logo requirements. These options give buyers a practical framework for describing how a custom product should differ from an existing style or supplied design.

A useful customization request identifies the preferred product type and explains which details need to be changed or developed. If a buyer has artwork, a label requirement, size information or a product reference, those materials can be included in the direct enquiry for discussion.

The available options should not be treated as automatic confirmation for every project. Yubei reviews the actual requirements provided by the buyer, so product details and cooperation conditions should be confirmed directly with the team.

Private Label

Private-Label Children's Clothing Cooperation

Buyers researching a private-label children's clothing manufacturer can discuss producing a supplied design and applying their own label with Yubei. The enquiry should explain the product, brand-label and customization requirements that need to be reviewed.

Private-label cooperation is relevant to brands and B2B buyers that want the finished product connected to their own identity. It does not replace the need to confirm project details: the buyer should send the information available so Yubei can discuss the actual request.

Buyer FAQ

OEM, ODM and Private-Label Questions

Verified answers about Yubei's custom children's clothing cooperation.

Does Yubei provide OEM children's clothing manufacturing?

Yes. Yubei can discuss producing children's clothing designs supplied by buyers and applying the buyer's own label as part of an OEM enquiry.

What is the difference between Yubei's OEM and ODM cooperation?

OEM cooperation can begin with a design supplied by the buyer. For ODM cooperation, Yubei's design team can discuss developing products around the buyer's specifications and market requirements.

What children's clothing customization options does Yubei publish?

Yubei's published customization options include fabric, color, size and printing or logo requirements. Buyers should describe the options relevant to their product enquiry.

Does Yubei support private-label children's clothing?

Yes. Buyers can discuss applying their own label to children's clothing as part of an OEM or private-label enquiry with Yubei.

Can buyers use Yubei's existing products as references?

Yes. Buyers can review Yubei's summer, autumn, winter and mixed collections, note relevant product model numbers and use those references when explaining their requirements.

What should buyers include in an OEM or ODM enquiry?

Buyers should share the available design or product concept, relevant model references, quantity, customization requirements, requested delivery date and destination information.

How can buyers contact Yubei about an OEM or private-label project?

Buyers can contact Yubei through the website Contact page, WhatsApp or email and include the information available for their project.
