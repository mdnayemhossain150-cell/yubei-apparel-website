Wholesale Kidswear RFQ Guide

Prepare the product, quantity, customization, delivery and shipping details a children's clothing supplier needs to review your request and develop a more accurate quotation.

Answer first: A useful kidswear RFQ identifies the product, required quantity, sizes, colors, customization, target date and destination. Add reference photos, material or packaging requirements when available.

What Is a Kidswear RFQ?

An RFQ, or request for quotation, is the information a buyer sends to a supplier when asking for pricing and commercial terms. For wholesale children's clothing, the request should explain both the product and the conditions under which the buyer needs it supplied.

A complete RFQ helps reduce assumptions. It allows the supplier to review the correct style, quantities, size range, customization work and delivery destination before preparing a quotation. Missing information may require follow-up questions and can delay an accurate response.

This buyer resource is provided by Yubei Apparel, legally Huzhou Zhili Yubei Clothing Co., Ltd., a children's clothing manufacturer, wholesaler and exporter based in Zhili, Huzhou, Zhejiang, China.

RFQ Essentials

Information Buyers Should Include

Send what is already known and mark any undecided details. The supplier can then see which requirements are fixed and which still need discussion.

Product or model

Provide the model number, product-page link or catalog reference. Buyers can start with Yubei's children's clothing catalog.

Reference photo or design

Attach a clear reference image, design file or sketch when the request is not based on an existing product.

Product type

State whether the request is for a dress, set, T-shirt, jacket, jeans or another children's clothing category.

Required quantity

Give the total quantity and, when possible, the quantity split by size, color or design.

Sizes

List the required size range and any size specification or measurement chart that should be reviewed.

Colors

Identify preferred colors and provide color references when an exact shade matters.

Fabric or material

Share known composition, weight, feel or performance requirements. If undecided, state that alternatives need discussion.

Printing or embroidery

Describe the technique, placement, dimensions and artwork available for any print or embroidery.

Private or custom label

Explain the required brand, care, size or hangtag labels and include specifications or artwork when available.

Packaging

State the required individual packaging, carton marking, barcode or other packing instructions.

Sample requirement

Say whether a sample is required and which product or custom details it should demonstrate.

Delivery and destination

Provide the target delivery date, destination country or port, and preferred shipping method if known.

Ready Stock vs Custom/OEM RFQ

The information needed depends on whether the buyer wants an existing stocked style or a custom-production project.

Quotation Preparation

How to Request a More Accurate Quotation

Quantity affects the production or stock allocation being reviewed. Product specifications define what must be sourced, developed or changed. Custom printing, embroidery, labels and packaging add project-specific work. The destination and shipping preference determine which logistics information is relevant.

For these reasons, a short request such as “send your best price” is usually not enough for an accurate comparison. Buyers should provide the same clear specification to each supplier and identify anything that is still undecided.

No price or production approval should be assumed until the actual RFQ has been reviewed.

Useful supporting files
  • Product-page link or model number
  • Reference photographs or design files
  • Size chart or measurements
  • Print, embroidery or label artwork
  • Packaging and carton instructions
Before You Send

RFQ Step-by-Step Checklist

Use this sequence to prepare a request that can be reviewed efficiently.

  1. Choose the request type.Confirm whether you need ready stock, OEM, ODM or another custom-production arrangement.
  2. Identify the product.Add a Yubei model or product link, or attach a reference design or photograph.
  3. Set the quantity.Provide the total quantity and the split by size and color when available.
  4. Describe specifications.List sizes, colors, fabric or material requirements and any measurement information.
  5. Explain customization.Include printing, embroidery, labels, packaging and sample requirements.
  6. Add delivery information.State the destination, target delivery date and shipping preference if known.
  7. Attach supporting files.Check that designs, artwork, size charts and reference images are readable.
  8. Review before sending.Mark undecided items clearly and include your contact details for follow-up.

Inquiry Process

What Happens After Sending an RFQ?

A clear request gives the team a practical starting point for reviewing the product and cooperation requirements.

1

Information review

2

Buyer FAQ

Wholesale Kidswear RFQ Questions

Concise answers to common questions buyers ask before requesting a quotation.

What is Yubei's MOQ for custom kidswear?

Yubei's standard custom-production MOQ is 300 pieces per model. Custom designs remain subject to technical review, material availability and the applicable MOQ.

Can I order only a few ready-stock pieces?

Yes. Ready-stock products can be purchased in any quantity, subject to current inventory. Include the model, sizes, colors and quantities you need so availability can be checked.

Does Yubei provide OEM and ODM?

Yes. Yubei provides OEM, ODM, private-label and sample-development support for children's clothing projects.

Can I add my own brand label?

Yes. Private-label requirements can be included in a custom-production RFQ. Explain the label type, placement and any available specifications.

Can I send a reference design or photo?

Yes. Send the reference design or photo with the product type, quantity and required changes. Custom designs are subject to technical review, material availability and the applicable MOQ.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Send the product or model, quantity, sizes, colors, known material requirements, customization, packaging, sample needs, destination and target delivery date.

Can Yubei arrange international shipping?

Yes. International shipping can be arranged by sea or air, including door-to-door shipping where applicable. Export-document handling is also available.

How quickly will Yubei respond to an inquiry?

Yubei normally responds to quotation requests within 24 hours. A response may request clarification; it does not guarantee final pricing or production approval within that period.
