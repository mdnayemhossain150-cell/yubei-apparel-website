Yubei Children's Clothing Manufacturer in Zhili

Huzhou Zhili Yubei Clothing Co., Ltd., also known as Yubei Apparel, is a children's clothing manufacturer and exporter based in Zhili Town, Huzhou, Zhejiang, China. The company presents seasonal kidswear collections and works with buyers through wholesale, OEM, ODM, custom design and private-label cooperation.

For businesses searching for a China kidswear manufacturer or a Huzhou children's clothing manufacturer, Yubei provides a direct way to review current styles, learn about its services and contact the team from one website. Buyers comparing a children's clothing manufacturer in China, or researching a Zhili children's clothing factory, can also use the About page to see Yubei's showroom, production environment and company information.
