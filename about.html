About Us

One-Stop Service for Global Clients

Meet Yubei Apparel, a children's clothing manufacturer and exporter located in Zhili, Huzhou—one of China's leading kidswear production centers.

Children's Clothing Manufacturing Built for Global Buyers

We specialize in providing a one-stop service for global clients, including OEM, ODM, custom design, sample processing, and bulk wholesale. Our commitment is to deliver high-quality, comfortable, and fashionable children's clothing to customers worldwide.

One-Stop ServiceWe handle everything from design concept to finished product delivery, ensuring a seamless experience.
Full-Season CollectionWe offer a comprehensive range of children's clothing designed to suit diverse seasonal needs.
Specialization in SetsOur expertise lies in high-quality children's clothing sets, a top-selling category in global markets.
Ready Stock & DeliveryWe maintain substantial ready stock to enable quick response to urgent orders and fast delivery.
